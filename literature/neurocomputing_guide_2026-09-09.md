# Neurocomputing Guide for Authors record

Source: <https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/neurocomputing/publish/guide-for-authors>

Retrieved through the official ScienceDirect page on 9 September 2026. This file records the submission-relevant points used for the manuscript package. The linked page remains the authoritative source if the journal updates its instructions.

## Scope and article type

- Neurocomputing publishes fundamental contributions in neurocomputing theory, practice and applications.
- A Regular article must be directly relevant to neural networks and/or learning systems.
- The journal does not accept a pure dynamical-systems, pure control, pure fuzzy-logic, pure image-processing or pure combination-of-existing-algorithms paper without direct neural-network or learning-system relevance.
- StableShift is prepared as a Regular article because its central contribution is a neural forecasting architecture and its bounded online learning rule.

## Peer review and declarations

- The journal uses a single-anonymized review process and normally seeks at least two reviewers after editorial assessment.
- The title page must identify one corresponding author and keep full contact details current.
- The submission declaration covers prior publication, simultaneous consideration, approval by all authors and duplicate publication.
- Authors must disclose competing interests and funding sources.
- Generative AI or AI-assisted technologies used in manuscript preparation must be declared. AI tools cannot be authors and cannot replace human judgement.

## Files and formatting

- Editable source files are required. LaTeX files are accepted and should be supplied together with the associated bibliography, figures and supplementary files.
- The manuscript should include a title page, abstract, keywords, highlights, article sections, tables, figure captions, data statement and code statement where applicable.
- Figures must be supplied in assessable form with captions. The journal screens images for inappropriate manipulation.
- Supplementary material is uploaded as a separate file and should be cited in the manuscript.
- The article structure should use clearly defined numbered sections and subsections.

## References

- Every in-text citation must appear in the reference list and every listed reference must be cited.
- Each reference must correspond to a real source and should include complete bibliographic metadata and a DOI when available.
- The page recommends numbered references in square brackets, with a consistent style at submission. The manuscript therefore uses `elsarticle-num`.

## Submission route

- Submit through the journal's online system at <https://submit.elsevier.com/NEUCOM>.
- The system creates a single review PDF from uploaded files. Editable LaTeX, bibliography, figures and supplementary files remain part of the submission package.
